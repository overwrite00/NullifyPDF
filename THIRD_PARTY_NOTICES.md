## ISTAT place names

`lexicon_data_it.py` contains data derived from the ISTAT publication
"Elenco dei comuni italiani" (version of 2026-02-21, source:
https://www.istat.it/classificazione/codici-dei-comuni-delle-province-e-delle-regioni/).

- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ (ISTAT legal notes:
  https://www.istat.it/note-legali/).
- Changes: names were normalised and lower-cased, bilingual names were split,
  names that are also ordinary words were marked as ambiguous.
- ISTAT does not endorse this project.

The file is generated by `scripts/build_lexicon_data.py`; the SHA-256 of the
source workbook is recorded in the generated module.

## Given names and surnames (Wikidata)

`lexicon_data_it.py` also contains Italian given names and surnames taken from
Wikidata (items that have an article on the Italian Wikipedia).

- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0), all structured data of Wikidata
  (https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing).
- The lists are small (a few hundred given names and about one thousand
  surnames) and are only used to *confirm* a name; a name missing from them
  never causes a hit to be rejected.
